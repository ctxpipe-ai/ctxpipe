# P3-5 Graph relevance scoring

Status: not started. Notes from other tickets:

- **From P2-4:** `n.degree`, `n.inDegree`, and `n.outDegree` count the raw `WorkspaceSignal` edges of the revision. The counts include expired edges and two edges with the same endpoints and predicate. Use them only as a cheap hub hint. Bounded reads and scores must count the edges that they actually walk, after the predicate, direction, validity, and confidence filters.
