# P3-6 `get_related`: notes from the P2-3 review

- The `score` of `searchKnowledge` is a reciprocal rank fusion sum with k = 60. The largest value is 2/61, about 0.033 (rank 1 in both rankings). A hit in one ranking only is at most 1/61, about 0.016. P3-5 expects `queryMatch` in 0..1. Scale the score before it enters the signal model: divide by the best score of the result set (the top hit gets 1), or by 2/61 for a value that is comparable across queries. Do not pass the raw score.
- Restart vector: the seeds (at most 10) and the query hits. `searchKnowledge` returns at most 50 hits, so at most 60 entries. 50 query hits are enough. At the RRF scale, rank 50 holds 1/110 against 1/61 for rank 1, so a hit below rank 50 adds almost no restart mass. Keep the limit at 50; do not raise it for `get_related`.
- `searchKnowledge` has no cursor. Pages come from the in-process cache of P3-6. Ties break by the chunk key (workspace, unit, chunk hash, occurrence), so the same query on the same projection gives the same order. `unitIds` is the explored set.
- Each hit now has `claims` (`predicate`, `to`).
