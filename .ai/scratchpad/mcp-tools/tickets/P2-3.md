# P2-3 Indexed hybrid knowledge search: notes from the P2-2 review

  - Local Compose Postgres: pgvector 0.8.2.
  - Neon production: not checked (the Neon MCP did not connect from the agent). Check with `SELECT extversion FROM pg_extension WHERE extname = 'vector'` and record the result here before P2-3 merges.
- Define the `validAt` filter. Proposal, with no new column: a unit matches `validAt` T when it has no claim with a validity window, or at least one claim with `validFrom` null or at most T and `validTo` null or after T (from `workspace_knowledge_units.claims`). Confirm the rule, or add a column, before the search SQL.
- Own the move of chat retrieval (`workspace-chat-retrieval.ts`) to chunk search. Then delete `workspace_knowledge_units.embedding` and the chunk-mean update in `persistUnitEmbeddings`.

## Inputs from P2-1 and P2-2

- Chunks: `workspace_knowledge_chunks` (`search` tsvector, `embedding`, `start_line`, `end_line`). Rows are the active projection only; join units on `(workspace_id, unit_id = serving_id)` for `kind`, `path`, `title`, `source`, `updated_at`.
- `start_line` and `end_line` are file lines, so a hit can give `read_file` a range.

## Result (build)

- `domain/workspaces/knowledge-search.ts` `searchKnowledge`: one SQL statement. A full-text ranking (chunks that match every query term first, then `ts_rank_cd` of any term) and an HNSW cosine ranking, each limited to 100 chunks, fuse with RRF (k = 60). The result is the best chunk of each unit, with `startLine`, `endLine`, the chunk text, and the unit fields. Filters: Workspaces, `kinds`, `pathPrefix` (literal, `starts_with`), `sources`, `updatedAfter`, `validAt`, `unitIds`. Units must be at the active SHA of their Workspace. A failed query embedding gives full-text results only (logged as a warning).
- The transaction sets `hnsw.iterative_scan = relaxed_order` and `hnsw.ef_search = 100`.
- `validAt` rule (as proposed): a unit matches T when it has no claim with a validity window, or when one such claim has `validFrom` null or at most T and `validTo` null or after T. ISO strings compare, as in P2-5. A claim with no window does not make a windowed unit match. Recorded in ADR-051.
- Chat `hybrid_search` calls `searchKnowledge` for its Workspace (8 hits, each `path (lines a-b)` and the chunk text). The org `objects` hits are gone. Removed: `workspace-chat-retrieval.ts` and its test, and the dead `hybridSearch`, `vectorSearch`, `bm25Search` modules over `objects`. Chat output no longer lists claims; `graph_lookup` gives them.
- Migration `20261013111410_drop_unit_embedding` drops `workspace_knowledge_units.embedding`. `persistUnitEmbeddings` is now `markKnowledgeEmbeddingsReady` (only the readiness check). The projection snapshot gives `embedded` (the unit has chunks and each has a vector) in place of the vector; the ingestion validator counts `!embedded`.

## Tests

`knowledge-search.integration.test.ts` (real Postgres as `ctxpipe_app`, msw embedding model): exact keyword first, paraphrase first through the embedding, title-only match, best chunk and line range, Workspace scope, each filter, the unit-id filter, another org sees nothing (both ways), embedding failure, chat `hybrid_search` output, and latency on 20,000 chunks with 2,000-dimension random vectors.

- Latency: local median of 5 runs 143 ms and 156 ms in two runs (pgvector 0.8.2, Apple silicon, Docker). Bound: 1,000 ms.
- The 20,000-chunk seed takes most of the test time, because each HNSW insert is serial (about 190 s in one transaction). Four parallel transactions share it now; the test takes about 67 s locally.
