# T22 Ownership migration and rollback config (CTX-640)

## What
- `ownershipMigrationFiles` (`apps/backend/src/domain/ingestion/ownership-migration.ts`): a pure transform. It moves old extracted files (`generated_by: ctxpipe` with an absolute `source` URL) to `generated_by: ctxpipe-extract` and `sources`, and removes `import_key`. The body stays byte for byte.
- The write-job kind `ownership_migration` and the workflow `workspace-write-ownership-migration`. It shares `defineKnowledgeRewriteWorkflow` with the import-key cleanup, which keeps its step names.
- `ingestionPublishPipeline()`: committed config, `legacy` until T21 merges. Hydrate plans the migration only for `v2`. The old pipeline skips its publish step for `v2`.

## Why
- The import-key cleanup removes `import_key` after the migration export, so the predicate cannot need `import_key`.
- No per-org or per-Workspace switch: a committed org list would put customer ids in a public repository, and a DB setting needs a new column.

## Tests
- Unit: `ownership-migration.test.ts`, `hydrate-write-planner.test.ts`, `write-jobs.test.ts`.
- Contract (real Postgres, real git): `write-ownership-migration-native.contract.test.ts`.

## Risks
- T21 must start ingestion v2 with `shadow: false` when the config is `v2`.
- A file that a human changed but kept `generated_by: ctxpipe` moves to ingestion v2. Its body and human sections stay.
