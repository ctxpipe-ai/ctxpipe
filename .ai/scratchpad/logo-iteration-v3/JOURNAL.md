# Pass 3 — brand personality, 10 × 4

Start: shipped pixel `ctx|`. Four new directions every loop. Pick one winner. Coherent pixel letters are the brand (not a TYPE fail). The pass-1 L-diagram stays the RED calibration.

| Loop | Four ways | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | heritage · fat cursor · stamp · **echo** | **D echo** | First bit of brand: teal offset on the shipped letters |
| 2 | echo+fat · **invert** · hard echo · echo stamp | **B invert** | Teal `ctx`, white pipe — color flip, still the same DNA |
| 3 | invert+fat · flag · C-pipe · **block cursor** | **D block** | Cursor becomes a mass, not a hairline |
| 4 | block+echo · **teal block** · notched · mark+word | **B teal block** | Accent back on the cursor; more iconic |
| 5 | echo+block · **chamfer** · plate · fleet | **B chamfer** | Ownable mark; fleet reads too much like a signal |
| 6 | **chamfer+echo** · deep cut · pixel type · negative | **A chamfer+echo** | Combines L1 personality with L5 mark |
| 7 | stack · all teal · **stair** · combo | **C stair** | Pixel-step cut is more “us” than a 45° |
| 8 | tight · **taller** · no echo · gap | **B taller** | Recovers the oversized pipe from the shipped mark |
| 9 | **color** · mono · ink block · darker light | **A color** | Teal block is the brand; mono is the merch drawing |
| 10 | **ref** · heavy · sharp · quiet echo | **A ref** | Assembled family: echo + chamfered teal block |

**Recommended:** shipped pixel `ctx` + teal echo + chamfered teal block cursor. Dark `#40E0D0` / light `#0F766E`. Square mark = the chamfer block. 16px = hinted chamfer.

**Bold alternates (also from this pass):** L2 invert (teal letters); L7 stair (pixel-step block).
