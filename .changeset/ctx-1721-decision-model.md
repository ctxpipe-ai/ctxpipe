The knowledge plane calls its decision model through the `decide()` activity of TanStack AI. A self-hosted install can set the decision model with `DECISION_MODEL_PROVIDER` (`openrouter` or `openai`), `DECISION_MODEL_NAME`, `DECISION_MODEL_URL` and `DECISION_MODEL_API_KEY`. With no decision setting, the decision model is Jev on OpenRouter, as before.
