This page is about **hosted ctxpipe**. On a self-hosted deployment, you choose
the providers and the retention. The model calls go from your deployment to the
provider that you configure, so ctxpipe the company never sees your prompts or
your model traffic.

## What ctxpipe reads

- The git repositories that you link to a Workspace, and the Workspace
  repository itself.
- The content of the sources that you add: Linear, Notion, Confluence, and
  PagerDuty mirrors, the closed pull requests of linked GitHub repositories,
  and the Slack threads that a person captures. ctxpipe writes this content
  into the Workspace repository, which you control, then indexes it.

ctxpipe reads nothing that you did not connect.

## What you control

- You create each Workspace, link each repository, and approve each source scope
  in a pull request. ctxpipe ingests nothing without one of these actions.
- You can unlink a repository, remove a source, or delete a Workspace at any
  time. This stops new indexing. The indexed data can stay until the deletion
  process of your organization is complete.
  [Get in touch](/docs/getting-started/get-in-touch) for the details.
- The data of your organization is separate from the data of other
  organizations.
- If you stop using ctxpipe, your Workspace repositories stay with you, with
  their full history.

## Sub-processors

| Provider | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Railway | Compute and hosting, and our own observability (logs, traces, and model traces) |
| Neon | Postgres database |
| OpenRouter | Model routing, only to zero data retention endpoints |
| Vercel | Isolated sandboxes (microVMs) for Workspace chat and for ingestion. A sandbox holds a copy of the Workspace repository, or of the linked repository that it ingests. |

## How ctxpipe uses models

- **Workspace chat.** Your messages, and the context that the agent retrieves
  (search results, file excerpts, and graph context), go to the model. Each
  conversation runs in its own sandbox. Its changes stay on a conversation
  branch until you open a pull request.
- **Ingestion.** ctxpipe uses models to extract knowledge from parts of the
  linked repositories, in an isolated sandbox. See
  [How indexing works](/docs/connections/ingestion).
- **MCP tools.** The tools call no model. They search and read your data. Your
  coding agent uses its own model.

ctxpipe does not send a whole repository to a model in one request. Retrieval
and tools select the context.

On hosted ctxpipe, each model call goes through OpenRouter to
[zero data retention (ZDR)](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/zdr)
endpoints only. Our OpenRouter account enforces this, so the model providers do
not store your prompts or train on them. See the
[OpenRouter privacy policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy).

## Retention

We do not publish fixed retention periods for indexed data yet. One exception:
ctxpipe deletes the saved sandbox files of a Workspace chat conversation 29 days
after the last use of the conversation. The branch of the conversation in your
repository stays. For deletion times, export, or a DPA,
[get in touch](/docs/getting-started/get-in-touch).

## What we do not do

- We do not train models on your content.
- We do not share data across organizations, and we do not sell it. The
  sub-processors above only operate the product.
- We do not ingest sources that you did not connect.

## Certifications

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 for ctxpipe are on the roadmap. Our infrastructure
sub-processors publish their own compliance and security documents.

For questions about your data, sub-processors, or contracts,
[get in touch](/docs/getting-started/get-in-touch).
